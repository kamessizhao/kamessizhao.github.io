Jiayu Kamessi Zhao

Assistant Professor of Operations, Information, and Technology, Stanford Graduate School of Business

I am an Assistant Professor of Operations, Information, and Technology at Stanford Graduate School of Business.

My research studies how service platforms can leverage operational policies and market design to improve efficiency. My recent work examines AI-enabled transportation and mixed-autonomy systems, including robotaxi fleet operations.

I completed my PhD in Operations Research at MIT and my undergraduate studies at Columbia University. In 2025–2026, I was a postdoctoral researcher at Waymo’s marketplace and supply optimization team.
